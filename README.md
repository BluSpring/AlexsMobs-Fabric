# Alex's Mobs (Fabric)
An unofficial Fabric port of Alex's Mobs, with actual permission from Alex himself to work on,
and with no AI used in its creation. Any issues found here are ethically and organically sourced.

## Why should I use your port over the 3 other ports that exist?
Well, for starters, if you don't like AI usage then here you go.<br>
But also, I've decided to actually actively maintain this codebase, and be generally mindful about
any crashes that occur. Mod compatibility is also a top priority here, which unfortunately the other ports 
do not seem to abide by.

What's weird is that all of these ports don't seem to realize that the original mod *has* an official
1.21.1 branch almost completely ready to go. They all completely port from scratch in 1.20.1 instead,
and never contribute back to the original mod.
My port on the other hand is based on the official branch. Any fixes here that would apply upstream, 
alongside any additional features, I'm actively contributing them there too. If I'm moving onto a newer version
of Minecraft, I'm contributing that update upstream too.

## You have permission from Alex?
Yeah. I've ported this mod before, back in 2023 for a Minecraft server called Crimecraft S3, which was Fabric 1.19.2.
Which, funny enough, makes me the first person to have ported this mod to Fabric apparently. And if you
don't count the AI ports, I am the only person to have done so.

After joining Alex's Discord at some point however, I realized that he had a clause for no porting, and I felt guilty.
But I was reached out to by someone who has known Alex for years, and he got me in contact with Alex to get actual
permission from him to work on this, and here we are.
![Screenshot of me getting permission from Alex](/gallery/permission.png)

## Where's Modrinth & CurseForge?
Following Alex's request from 2024, this port is not currently available on Modrinth and CurseForge. However, due to the current
circumstances, I'm currently awaiting for a response from him to properly publish this there, because quite frankly, these
low quality ports being published has gone out of control, and I would rather have an actually high quality
version of the mod available for users to use, and also help other mod developers who have had headaches trying to
support these low quality ports too.
